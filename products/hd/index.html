IP67 Waterproof

The HD series is waterproof, both from the front and from the back. While you might get occasionally splashed out at sea, there is no need to worry about a bit of rain or saltwater ruining your day.

Although the body is made of steel, the coat of paint we've applied is so good that we've had bow-mounted units going out every day for a year and still coming back spotless.

Turn Up The Brightness

The HD series goes up to 1400 nits of brightness thanks to the sturdy steel construction. See your charts or sounder even when there's not a cloud in the sky.
