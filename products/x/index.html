X Series

Our flagship X Series is made for permanent outdoors mounting. With best-in-class IPX8 certification, an all-aluminium body and integrated heat sinks, the X Series will handle all conditions you might face at sea.

For fishing applications, the X Series is specificially designed for bow mounting. Connect it to your chartplotter to get live sonar up front, or add on a Garmin LiveScope to get Fish TV on a big screen.

IPX8 Waterproof, Everywhere

The X Series goes above and beyond our other ranges with IPX8 certification to better withstand the marine environment. Not just the front panel, but the entire body and connectors too. They are made to survive being submerged underwater, not just a little splash.

Full-Body Aluminium Enclosure

With a body CNC machined from a single block of aluminium, the X series is highly corrosion resistant and will handle anything nature throws at it for years to come.

Turn Up The Brightness

Thanks to large integrated heat sink array, we are able to offer some of the brightest displays available at 1500 nits, with applied anti-glare coating.

No need to worry about temperatures under direct sunlight, just set the brightness all the way up.
