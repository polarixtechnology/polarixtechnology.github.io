About POLARIX

Born on the Reef

Our story begins in Cairns, gateway to the Great Barrier Reef. Our founder, Wallace, wanted a bigger screen than the 12 inch chartplotter on his center console, but wasn't willing to spend 5-figure sums on one.

"I'll do it myself", he thought, having already been designing and crafting his own line of artisanal fishing lures, and thus, the first Polarix unit was born.

Spreading Throughout Australia

Out on the reef and at the boat ramp, people noticed the HD24 on his console and asked about it. Through word of mouth, Polarix evolved from a passion project into a homegrown Australian business, and is now mounted on boats all over Queensland and the Northern Territories, from small bass boats to fishing charters.

With early customer feedback, we developed our X Series lineup, the world's first large, high brightness, weatherproof and waterproof high definition touchscreen, ideal for bow-mounted use with live sonar and other outdoor applications.

Debut on the World Stage

Polarix was brought to Singapore for the Asia Pacific Maritime Expo in 2026, where we put up the humblest of booths for our first ever exhibition. Despite having nothing more than our screens on display, we received great interest from the commercial marine industry as a new, innovative product.

We are continuing to grow and develop new ranges of screens for all of marine and hobby fishing, making big and capable screens accessible with a small budget.
