Upgrade Your Fishing

Don't squint at your small fishfinder or live sonar from far looking for the next catch, spot them with ease or plot your route on a big screen. Our range of weatherproof touchscreens are made for mounting anywhere on your boat, whether it's the bow, stern, or even on your console.

Add a Polarix X22 beside your Garmin LiveScope to see what you're pointing at. It is made with aluminium for complete saltwater tolerance, and has integrated heat sinks for temperature control under the hot sun. Additionally, pair it with a Phase Pole for maximum ease of control.

Prefer to have a bigger main screen? Replace your chartplotter with a Polarix HD18T, giving you the best access to your charts and data at the helm. Your chartplotter can then be mounted facing the bow for access from the fishing deck.

For Big Boats Too

Our screens are not just for small dinghys. Have a sportfisher, or fish from a yacht? Mount a Polarix screen at the stern so you and your passengers can see - and control - the chartplotter while fishing.

Having a quiet day? Switch to the second HDMI input for entertainment!
